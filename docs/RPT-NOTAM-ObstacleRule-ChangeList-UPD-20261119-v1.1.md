## How the list was made

- **NavBlue/eOFP:** the whole OFP text of each fixture, split as TC-171-030 splits it (v1.13.0 and v1.14.0 split it identically). Each NOTAM evaluated alone, with no window (`etaMin` null: in effect) and at the TC-171-030 sweep (ORIG and DEST; ETA 0000, 0059, 0500, 1000, 1159, 1200, 1640, 2300, 2359Z; departure unknown, 0000Z, 1140Z; with and without the flight date). The whole-text split also yields pieces that are not one NOTAM (the OFP text before the first NOTAM, or a last NOTAM that runs on into the weather); they are listed with a note, as the corpus holds them. In the app each airport card reads its own NOTAM text, not these pieces.
- **FlightKeys:** each airport's `notamsRaw` (the NOTAM section as printed, type headings included) from the FlightKeys parser v0.3.0. v1.14.0 reads `notamsRaw` as it is: `_splitNotams()` cuts out the headings and every item carries its heading as `fkCategory`. v1.13.0 reads the same items without headings (the parser's `notams` text). Each item alone, with no window and at each of its airport's windows (the briefing's role windows, at their midpoint).
- **Groups.** B and C compare the result with no window (the obstacle rule does not depend on time). U is every obstacle NOTAM v1.14.0 shows because the patterns cannot decide, whatever v1.13.0 did. G is an obstacle NOTAM shown by both whose result differs: the O-25 card group it is now given. **Card group** = where the item lands on the card with no window, as destination (DEST) and as departure airport (ORIG).
- The same NOTAM printed in several packages is listed once per package.

## What remains in U, and why

D67a decides an obstacle NOTAM by whether a procedure (runway, approach, SID, STAR, departure, missed approach, circling) is affected. What is left undecided, and shown:

| Pattern | Rows | Why it stays undecided |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| `SURFACE_PENETRATION` | 22 | the text says a protection surface (transitional, inner horizontal, OLS …) is penetrated or infringed: a fact that may affect the approach, missed approach or circling, but no procedure or effect is stated |
| `SURFACE_NAMED` | 33 | the obstacle stands in or above a named surface (approach, transitional, horizontal, conical, OFZ): the surface protects a procedure, but no procedure or effect is stated |
| `RUNWAY_STRIP` | 1 | an obstacle in the runway strip / graded area / RESA: the runway itself may be affected, no effect stated |
| `TAKEOFF_SIDE_UNSTATED` | 2 | D67a O-26 (Exec): the approach side is stated not affected (OCA/H NOT AFFECTED), a height is given near a runway and the take-off side is not stated |
