## New fields

Five top-level extensions, in this order, after `operatingPilots` and before `sections` (`EXTENSION_FIELDS` of `ofp-flightkeys` has 19 entries).

| Field | Content | Explicit empty | Source decision |
| :---- | :------ | :------------- | :-------------- |
| `firNotams[]` | One record per FIR block printed in the NOTAM Briefing, in route order: `{ name, code, empty, items }`. `name`: the block's name line as printed (`EDMONTON FIR`, `CHICAGO ARTCC LOW` …); `code`: the ICAO code of its `FIR<ICAO>` line. `items[]`, in printed order: `{ id, category, origin, text, source }` — `id` read by the NOTAM ruleset (`_fkHeader`); `category` the printed type heading the item sits under (§A.6), `''` if none (the heading is never in `text`); `origin` `'NOTAM'`, or `'COMPANY'` for a Company NOTAM keyed to this FIR (O-40: a `GENERAL` Company item whose SUBJECT key `IIII-` is the block's code; the item is also in `companyNotams[]`); `text` the item as printed, line breaks and indentation kept (D54), page and column breaks removed; `source` the printed positions as in `notamItems` (addendum 3). `empty`: the block has no item (it prints the empty-state box). Items printed under more than one FIR block (the same ID and the same text): kept only under the blocks whose FIR is on the OFP — the FIR lines of the release navigation log and the FIRs of FPL field 18 `EET/` — and left out under the others; none of its FIRs on the OFP → left out under every block; one INFO lists every copy left out (O-49, amending O-41). A NOTAM printed under one block only is unchanged. `empty` keeps its meaning (the block prints the empty-state box). | `[]` (no NOTAM Briefing, or no FIR block) | **O-40, O-41, O-49** (Exec, 19 Nov); D54 |
| `firRaw[]` | One record per FIR block printed in the NOTAM Briefing, in route order: `{ name, code, raw }`. `raw`: the block exactly as printed, for the RAW presentation — the type headings in printed order with their centring, each item under its heading, line breaks and indentation, the empty-state box as printed; page and column breaks and the running header and footer removed (D54). The block's own two lines (name, `FIR<ICAO>`) are the `name` and `code`, not part of `raw`. No Company item. Never changed by O-49: every repeat stays as printed. | `[]` | **D64** (Exec, 18 Nov): "firRaw per FIR" |
| `natMessages[]` | Every NAT track message printed on the package's `NAT-TRACKS` pages, in printed order: `{ tmi, direction, valid, parts, complete, pages, text, selected }`. `tmi`: from `TMI IS nnn`, `null` if not readable; `direction`: `'EAST'` / `'WEST'` from the tracks' `EAST LVLS` / `WEST LVLS` lines, `null` if not readable; `valid`: the validity line as printed (`NOV 04/0100Z TO NOV 04/0800Z`, UTC); `parts`: N of `(NAT-n/N …`; `complete`: parts 1 … N all printed; `pages`: `[first, last]` PDF page; `text`: the message as printed across its pages, the `NAT-TRACKS` page heading removed; `selected`: the message passed to the NAT parser for `natTrack` (O-42). | `[]` (no NAT pages) | **O-43** (Exec, 19 Nov): "natMessages[] = every message; natTrack = the chosen one" |
| `hazards[]` | One record per FIR block of the Hazard Briefing (the Weather Briefing document after its airport blocks), in printed order: `{ name, code, text, empty }`. `text`: the block exactly as printed (the `SIGMET` sub-heading, the SIGMET / AIRMET / TWC products, the empty-state box), not split, not evaluated, repeats not merged. `empty`: the block prints no product. Never airport weather, `weatherRaw` or E-WINS. | `[]` | **O-45** (Exec, 19 Nov); display RAW only, collapsed by FIR unless expanded (step 5e) |
| `dispatchContact` | Whether crews must contact Dispatch, from the Dispatch contact line printed in the Company NOTAMs (the P0110-type item, HSCN and Company): `{ label, text }` — `label` `'REQUIRED'` for `Contact Dispatch for a preflight briefing.`, `'IF QUESTIONS'` for `Contact Dispatch for a preflight briefing if you have any questions.`; both printed → `'REQUIRED'`; `text` the line of the label, as printed. The lines also stay in the item's text in `companyNotams[]` exactly as printed. | `null` (neither line printed, or no Company NOTAMs read) | **O-46** (Exec, 19 Nov) |

## Core fields read in v0.4.0 (meaning unchanged, FK-P)

| Field | FlightKeys value |
| :---- | :--------------- |
| `enrouteNotams.fir` | The FIR blocks of `firNotams[]` with items (after O-49), in route order, separated by a blank line: the line `NAME (CODE)`, then the block's items as printed (no type heading); a Company item (O-40) after a `[COMPANY NOTAM]` line. Empty blocks are left out (INFO). `route`, `airway`, `waypoint`, `geographic` are `''` (FlightKeys prints en-route NOTAMs per FIR only, §A.8; INFO). |
| `natTrack` | The NAT parser's result (`options.parseNatTrack`, the app's `src/nat.html`) for the selected message (O-42: the flight's direction first, then the higher TMI); `assignedId` = the filed track from the OFP page 1 route and the ICAO FPL strip route only, never remarks or NOTAM text (O-43, O-47); the two differ → the FPL letter + crew-alert WARN. `null` without the NAT parser, without NAT pages, or when the NAT parser reads no Atlantic crossing. PACOTS: `null`, no track object (O-44). |
| `airportData[icao].curfew` | NavBlue's form (a string, one curfew line per line), from the `IIII-CURFEW` Company NOTAM only (O-37); UTC only (O-35); lines by aircraft type through a closed table of families and variants (O-33, O-48: a family name applies to every variant, a variant name to that variant only; a planned FK-P exception); a note line with no digits when no line names the type (O-33), the season dates are not stated (O-34) or the season changes in the flight month (O-34). The key is present only when a curfew is read. |
| `airportData[icao].opsNotes` | NavBlue's form `{ header, body }`: `header` `'IIII COMPANY NOTAMS'`; `body` every Company item located at the airport (`SUBJECT:` line + text), except the CURFEW item(s) and the items that repeat the airport's OFP Remarks (O-38, O-39). Present only when there is such an item and the airport has an entry. |
| `airportData[icao].notams` | v0.4.0 keeps the printed type heading lines, in printed order, before their items (D67; NOTAM v1.14.0 §5b reads them). Addendum 3's "record *i* of `notamItems` is piece *i* of `_splitNotams(notams)`" now reads: record *i* is item piece *i* of NOTAM v1.14.0's `_splitNotams(notams)` (a heading piece is not an item), i.e. piece *i* of `_splitNotams` of the text without the heading lines. |
| `companyNotams[]` (extension, contract 2.0) | Every Company NOTAM as printed, HSCN first: `{ id, location, subject, start, end, severity, origin, text, source }` — `location` the printed location or `'GENERAL'`; `severity` `'high'` (HSCN) / `'normal'`; `origin` `'HSCN'` / `'COMPANY'` (the source flag, O-40); `text` with line breaks (D54); `source` the printed positions. |

## Placement

`EXTENSION_FIELDS` (19): `revision`, `companyNotams`, `ofpRemarks`, `fplEquipment`, `catIiIiiRestrictions`, `securityMessages`, `extraFuel`, `pcf`, `permits`, `designator`, `swa`, `operatingPilots`, **`firNotams`, `firRaw`, `natMessages`, `hazards`, `dispatchContact`**, `sections`, `findings`. On an airport entry, `curfew` and `opsNotes` (core keys) follow `weatherRaw` when present.

## Open (for the Exec)

As with addenda 1–3, the locked NavBlue parser (contract 2.0, D47) does not return these fields. Under the Exec decision of 17 Nov ("ignore NavBlue" for FlightKeys-only extensions), the app must treat a missing `firNotams` / `firRaw` / `natMessages` / `hazards` as the explicit empty `[]`, and a missing `dispatchContact` as `null`.
